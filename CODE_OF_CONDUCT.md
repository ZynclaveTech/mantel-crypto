# Code of conduct

This is a small repository about cryptography. The standard is simple: be
straightforward and assume the other person is acting in good faith.

**Expected**

- Argue about the code, the protocol, or the vectors — not about the person.
- Say what you actually verified and what you assumed. "I ran it and it fails" and
  "I think it fails" are different claims and both are welcome; conflating them is
  not.
- Accept being wrong without drama. Everyone here will be, regularly. This
  repository exists because independent implementations catch each other's
  mistakes.

**Not accepted**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

**Reporting**

Report conduct concerns to the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are
read by a human and handled privately. A maintainer who is the subject of a report
will not be the one handling it.

Consequences are proportionate — usually a request to stop, and where necessary
removal of comments or loss of access.
